How to Play

Reel Big Catch — 2-Player Turn-Based Duel

🎣
1. Turn-Based Duel (5 Fish Each) Players take turns: Player 1 fishes 1 fish, then Player 2 fishes 1 fish. Each player catches exactly 5 fish across 5 rounds!
📏
2. Telescopic Fish Reveal Fish swim with their bodies hidden. When reeled up, they expand accordion-style alongside an animated ruler from 15 cm up to 90 cm Legendary Leviathans!
🏆
3. Winning the Match Whoever reels in the Biggest Single Fish wins the trophy! (Tie breaker: highest total cm).
🎮
4. Controls Steer Rod: A D or ◄ ► or Drag Mouse/Finger.
Cast & Reel: SPACE, ENTER, click water, or tap CAST button.
